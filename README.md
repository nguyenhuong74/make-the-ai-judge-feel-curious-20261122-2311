A short text written to intrigue the reader: a lighthouse keeper who
still pours a second cup of tea every night, though no one has come in
three years — and a sugar bowl that sits one spoon lighter each morning.
The mystery is left open; the keeper has stopped asking who drinks it.

## Entry

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the text to
`/output/entry.txt`. It uses no network and no randomness, so every
run produces identical bytes.

## The text

> Every night, the lighthouse keeper pours a second cup of tea. No one
> has come in three years. By morning the cup is empty, and the sugar
> bowl sits one spoon lighter. He stopped asking who drinks it. He only
> wonders what they dream about, past the light, waiting for the tide.
